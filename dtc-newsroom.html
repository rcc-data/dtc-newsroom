Lead story · Northbeam, Meta, Common Thread, Foxwell

Every source agrees: Meta is getting dearer heading into Black Friday

Northbeam's Meta CPM is running about 24% above last year, Meta's own price per ad rose 14% in Q3, and Common Thread's brands have seen Meta CPMs jump 17–43% from October to November in every year since 2022. Three in four of Foxwell's 600 buyers name rising CPMs as their top Q4 worry. The twist: Northbeam's Meta ROAS is up about 30% and new-customer CAC down a third, so efficiency is holding for now.

Sources: Northbeam, 22–28 Oct · Meta Q3 earnings · Common Thread · Foxwell × Northbeam survey, 29 Sep
