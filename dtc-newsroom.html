Notes on the data
  • Northbeam's samples change between posts (all accounts, accounts present in both periods, one-day clicks, accrual or cash). January and February 2026 appear twice with different values for this reason.
  • Weekly index weeks run Saturday–Friday until mid-April 2026 and Sunday–Sunday after. Not published: 29 Dec – 2 Jan, 31 Jan – 6 Feb, 14 – 21 Jun 2026. No weekly index ran Oct – mid-Dec 2025, so BFCM 2025 comes from the Cyber Week reports.
  • The week of 7–14 Jun 2026 was published twice (18 and 25 Jun); the later version is used.
  • Snapchat CPC shows +12.25% in four consecutive weeks (28 Feb – 27 Mar 2026), likely a carry-over error in Northbeam's graphic.
  • Until w/e 10 May 2026, budget share excludes "All other platforms"; the share desk rebases every week to the eight core platforms so the year is comparable.
  • Meta's average first-time CAC fell 33% YoY in Q3 while the median account's new CAC rose in most spend bands. Benchmark against your own spend band.

Earnings desk · Quarterly ad price check

Meta's price per ad is rising fastest in the US, at +23% a year

Each quarter Meta reports how many ad impressions it served and how much the average ad cost, both year on year. It is a market-wide read on auction inflation, covering every advertiser on Meta, to set against Northbeam's DTC sample.

Volume vs price, quarter by quarter

Lines are YoY % changes Meta reports in each release. When impressions grow fast (new placements such as Threads and Reels, and growth in Asia-Pacific), the average price is held down. When impression growth slows, price takes over: the second half of 2026 is that pattern.
