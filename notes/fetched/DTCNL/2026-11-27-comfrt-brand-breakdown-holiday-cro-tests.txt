🎨 Getting a little more Comfrt-able with conversion
Anyone else tempted to spend the rest of the weekend wrapped up in a giant blanket?
Comfrt has done a pretty impressive job of building an entire brand around that feeling. From oversized hoodies to matching sets and blankets, there's a lot to get cozy in. When we reviewed the site, its Dreamer Blanket alone had over 6,000 reviews.
We've had Comfrt on our list of brands to break down for a while, and Black Friday felt like a fitting time to share what the
Pilothouse
CRO team spotted.
Given that it's Black Friday, we wouldn't recommend making any big changes to your store this weekend. We've got a few things you can keep an eye on as orders come in, and some CRO ideas to tuck away for December.
1. Give those reviews a little more airtime
When the Pilothouse team reviewed Comfrt's homepage, the brand was already in Black Friday mode with an up to 80% off offer taking center stage.
For someone who's been waiting to get their hands on a Comfrt hoodie, that's probably all they need to see. Someone discovering the brand through a Black Friday ad might need a little more convincing.
And Comfrt has plenty to work with here. Thousands of product reviews, clinician endorsements, and some impressive numbers around how many products it's sold.
In the mockup above, Pilothouse brought some of that social proof further up the page, adding a review line above the Shop Black Friday button and moving the existing clinician endorsement directly below the hero.
Next time you're planning a big acquisition campaign, have a look at your homepage through the eyes of someone who's never heard of you. How far do they have to scroll before they find a reason to trust you?
2. I'll take the blanket in… actually, give me a minute
When we looked at Comfrt's Dreamer Blanket, there were more than 40 colors to choose from. Its Minimalist Hoodie? More than 30.
For those of us who struggle to pick between three paint samples, this could take a while.
And imagine trying to narrow those options down when you're shopping for someone else.
Once BFCM is over, have a look at which colors sold best. Pilothouse would test pulling three or four of those to the front, with the option to expand the selection and browse the rest.
You could also have some fun with the labels here. Which color do people buy most often as a gift? Is there a seasonal favorite? Are certain colors consistently more popular?
If you've got the sales data to back it up, give shoppers a little guidance. And make sure the colors you're recommending are actually in stock, especially over the holidays.
3. What if I get their size wrong?
It's probably one of the biggest things that makes people hesitate when buying clothing for someone else.
When we reviewed Comfrt's product pages, its standard 30-day return policy was tucked away in an accordion further down the page. Customers could return their purchases for store credit or make an even exchange, subject to availability.
We'd love to see those details right underneath the Add to Cart button, especially when so many people are shopping for gifts.
Comfrt's site also lists an
extended 45-day return window
for orders placed between November 1 and December 25. That's particularly useful information for someone buying a hoodie today that won't be opened until Christmas morning.
If you've extended your own return window for the holidays, double-check where you're communicating that. Someone deciding between two sizes on a PDP might never make it to your dedicated returns page.
4. A blanket AND cozy socks? Well, alright.
When the team added a Dreamer Blanket to their cart, there was quite a bit of room in Comfrt's cart drawer for a little something extra.
So Pilothouse mocked up a recommendation for a pair of socks priced at $9, with the option to select a color and add them without leaving the cart.
We like this pairing. A blanket and a pair of cozy socks would make a pretty nice Christmas gift, and it's easy to see someone spending a few extra dollars to complete it.
Once the weekend is over, pull your BFCM order data and see what people bought together. You might find some combinations you hadn't thought to merchandise together before.
Test one of those pairings in your cart drawer and track how often shoppers add it, what happens to AOV, and whether checkout completion holds up.
5. School's out. It's Black Friday.
One little thing that caught our eye during the review was a Back to School link hanging around in Comfrt's mobile menu.
We'd happily give that spot to a Gift Guide for the next few weeks.
Take a look at your own mobile menu today. Are there any old campaign links hanging around? How easy is it for shoppers to get to your Black Friday offers, bestselling products, and gifts?
As orders come in this weekend, keep a running list of anything you notice about how people are shopping or where they're getting stuck. You'll have a good starting point for your December CRO tests.
For now, if everything's running smoothly, leave it be. Happy selling!
