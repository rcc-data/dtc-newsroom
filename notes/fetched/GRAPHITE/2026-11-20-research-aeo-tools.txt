As of November 2026, there are at least 81 dedicated answer engine optimization (AEO) tools and 14 marketing platforms with AEO features.
The 19 AEO tool companies that have disclosed their funding have raised at least $594.3M.
This page provides a list of AEO tools, their entry pricing, publicly disclosed funding, and what unbiased Reddit users say about them.
Of the 11 tools with enough opinions to score, Peec AI, Otterly AI, and SE Ranking have the highest Reddit Recommendation Scores (66 out of 100).
We flag Reddit users who are likely promoting a product, explicitly or implicitly, and exclude their opinions. Of the users who gave an opinion of an AEO tool, we identify 55% as likely promoters.
The most discussed aspects of tools on Reddit are price, the accuracy of visibility numbers, and the completeness and usefulness of citation data.

Reddit Opinions and Scores
To understand what Reddit users think about AEO tools, we identify the 54 subreddits where AEO is regularly discussed. In these subreddits, 5,990 posts and 25,392 comments from January to September 2026 mention a tool. We classify whether each
unbiased
opinion of a tool encourages or discourages using it, and average these into the tool's Reddit Recommendation Score, from 0 to 100. A score of 100 means every opinion strongly encourages using the tool, 0 means every opinion strongly discourages it, and 50 is neutral. Most favorable opinions are not strong recommendations, so a tool that everyone recommended without strong praise would score 75. The
Data and Methodology
section describes how we identify opinions and compute the score in full.

Undisclosed Promotion on Reddit
Of the 2,365 users who gave an opinion of an AEO tool or a platform's AEO features, we flag 1,298 (55%) as likely working for or promoting a marketing software product. We exclude their opinions. In a sample of 43 flagged users that we reviewed, 12% disclosed that they were promoting a product, 62% promoted one without saying so, and we judged the rest not to be promoters.
Below, we review some patterns of promotion in flagged accounts. Each pattern spans several posts or comments, which our classification considers together, and the classification flags all 41 accounts in these examples. We replace the name of each promoted product with [tool].
Inconsistent details:
An account recommended the same lesser-known tracker in eight posts and comments. In January it wrote as a customer who had switched from Peec. Eleven days later it posted as a prospective buyer.
"We use [tool] for AEO monitoring… Before that, we used Peec and other alternatives but found it pretty lacking, hence the switch."
"Right now I am looking at Profound, but I am not willing to pay up to $400 just for it… [tool] looks great at just a fourth of that price."
Borrowed credibility:
An account listed a little-known tool alongside well-known trackers in four comments over eight days.
"Then track AI answers with tools like Profound, Otterly, or [tool]."
Rapid posts:
An account posted three recommendations of one product within 12 minutes.
"Lately I've been using [tool] as part of that process, and it's been surprisingly helpful."
Suspiciously similar language:
Over six weeks, 38 accounts recommended the same product in 52 comments, each using the phrase "from what I've read." The phrase appears with any other tool no more than twice.
"From what I've read, people seem to really like [tool] for helping with this."
What Reddit Users Praise and Criticize
To help buyers evaluate AEO tools, we identify the aspects Reddit users most often praise and criticize across 1,557
unbiased

Product Features
Reddit Praise and Criticism of AEO Tool Features
Source:
Graphite analysis of Reddit, Jan–Sep 2026. We include posts and comments from the 54 subreddits where AEO is regularly discussed. We identify the aspects users praise and criticize across 1,557 opinions of 44 tools. One opinion can praise or criticize several aspects. We exclude opinions from users likely working for or promoting a marketing software product.
Measurement accuracy:
Tools report how often a brand appears in AI answers, usually as a visibility score, and a user can only act on that score if it is accurate. Opinions of its accuracy are mostly negative (27 favorable, 143 unfavorable), and no other feature draws as many unfavorable ones. Because AI answers vary between runs, users, and locations, many treat any visibility score as an estimate. One wrote, "there is no way for you to know how accurate they are." Others noticed that tools disagree with each other: "[Two other tools] show different data too, but my opinion is that none of them tracks this well." Several advise users to "treat them as trend, not truth" and to check the numbers by hand. Favorable opinions often carry the same caveat, as in "pairing [the tool's] citations with periodic manual checks at least gives you something real to point to." In a
separate study
, we describe how to estimate visibility reliably.
Citation data:
The pages that AI answers cite as sources show which of a brand's own pages to improve and where else it could get mentioned. Opinions of this citation data are mostly positive (103 favorable, 52 unfavorable). Users say that checking answers by hand does not provide this: "The thing the manual approach can't give you is the source layer." The criticism is mostly that the data does not match what users see in their own analytics, as in "it shows that my site doesn't appear in ChatGPT, but I get most traffic from ChatGPT," or that the data is too noisy to use: "the citations and queries it gives me are largely useless."
Recommendations:
Some tools suggest changes to a site or its content that could improve its visibility in AI answers, which a score alone does not tell a user. Opinions of these recommendations are mixed (64 favorable, 88 unfavorable). Some users find the advice generic: "The suggested optimisations are things a normal SEO would know." Others want an explanation of why a page is cited: "[The tool] tells me I appeared but not why a specific page got pulled over others." Praise goes to tools that suggest what to do next, as in "[Both tools] have way more than just visibility tracking - they can actually help you figure out what to do next."
Data detail and access:
A score alone does not show why it is high or low, or why it changed. Explaining it requires the prompts, answers, and cited pages behind it, and exports let a user analyze the data elsewhere or move it to another tool. Opinions of this access are mixed (54 favorable, 73 unfavorable). Users praise a report where "by the end you're at the actual URLs instead of clicking through yet another aggregate." Users also criticize limited exports: "there's not even a good way to export useful data sheets." One user warned that without exports "its increasingly difficult to switch app over time."
Marketing platform inclusion:
Some marketing platforms offer AI visibility tracking alongside their other products, which saves a team that already uses one from buying and learning another tool. Opinions of these features are mostly positive (95 favorable, 27 unfavorable). The praise is about that convenience, as in "the CRM context for setup is genuinely useful if you're already in that ecosystem." The criticism is that the features are shallower than in dedicated tools: "a lot of the AI visibility features feel bolted on right now."
Pricing and Support
Reddit Praise and Criticism of AEO Tool Pricing and Support
Source:
Graphite analysis of Reddit, Jan–Sep 2026. We include posts and comments from the 54 subreddits where AEO is regularly discussed. We identify the aspects users praise and criticize across 1,557 opinions of 44 tools. One opinion can praise or criticize several aspects. We exclude opinions from users likely working for or promoting a marketing software product.
Price and value:
It is not yet clear how much visibility in AI answers is worth to a business, which makes it hard to know whether a tool justifies its cost. Users judge price more often than any other aspect, and opinions are mostly negative (98 favorable, 182 unfavorable). One user who tried two of the better-known tools called them "extremely expensive and no ROI" and advised others to "go for a monthly plan to test waters." Praise is mostly relative, for a tool that costs less than the alternatives, as in "Not perfect (and no Claude in it) - but cheaper than [another tool]."
Add-on cost and price scaling:
How a tool charges can matter as much as its entry price. An add-on raises the cost of a platform a team already pays for, and a price per prompt or project grows with the number of brands or clients tracked. Opinions of paying extra for AI visibility as an add-on are mostly negative (22 favorable, 66 unfavorable), as are opinions of how a price grows with use (5 favorable, 47 unfavorable). One user wrote, "It's a separate add-on that needs to be purchased on top of the base plan." For agencies with many clients, a price per project can make a tool impractical: "[One tool] is out for me because I'm working across too many projects." Praise goes to plans that include AI visibility tracking, as in "I also like that the tracking is included from the first plan rather than needing a separate addon just to get started."
