NATSEC Roundtable No. 17: Nashville and Peter Thiel
https://2pml.com/2026/10/20/nashthiel/
2026-10-20

Peter Thiel closed his four-part Nashville lectures on October 19th with three tests for the empire that holds back the end of the world. He called the second test scripture. It is a payments thesis, and the operators reading this are already standing on the rails.
The lecture was titled “The New Rome,” the fourth of four closed-door sessions hosted by
The Pamphleteer
and Meriwether Academy at $100 a seat, with devices prohibited and the room asked to keep what it heard inside the building.
Nathan Worcester
reported from the room anyway, and his account,
relayed in full
within a day, is the most precise record of the night that exists. What follows leans on it, on the recordings of the first two nights that Politico obtained, and on an attendee’s account of the third.
The series ran September 28 through October 19, opening at the Grand Masonid Lodge of Tennessee on Broadway, and the first three nights built the frame the fourth one needed. Thiel cast AI regulation as the on-ramp to a one world government and
accused Obama and Pope Leo
of leveraging the technology’s unpopularity to boost their own, he spent a stretch of one night on effective altruism, which he said had brainwashed a share of the people building the models, and on night three he named the
katechon
of 2 Thessalonians 2, the restrainer that holds back the man of lawlessness, and
suggested the great fortunes
had a role in being it. The force to be restrained, in that telling, was globalism, with the ICC, USAID, and the SWIFT system named outright.
This is the seventeenth dispatch in the
NATSEC series
and the first one built on a sermon. It earns the slot because the sermon was about infrastructure.
BLUF
The military test is not close. The FY2027 request puts national defense at
$1.5 trillion
, 4.6 percent of GDP and the highest single-year level since World War II, and the Iran war that emptied the magazine we wrote about in
August
is the reason the number is that large.
The financial test is where the margin is thinnest and still decisive. The dollar is
56.7 percent
of allocated reserves as of the second quarter, against a 20 percent euro and a 2 percent renminbi, and it still carries
half of all SWIFT payment value
while CIPS and mBridge grow from a small base.
The ideological test has no clean number, and Thiel answered it with geography. He named Nashville the center of a religion without a center and told the room to act like it.
The position of this brief: Thiel put Revelation 13:17 beside SWIFT and the reserve dollar and did not flinch, and the reading holds. The rails that restrain are the rails that would license, which makes payments infrastructure the national security question that commerce operators already sit on.
What Thiel said in the room
He opened with cities, which he treats as something nearer to the principalities and powers of Ephesians 6:12 than to any human institution, because they outlast the companies and the people inside them. He watched Mark Zuckerberg accept, from the Facebook board, that the company would one day die, and he called it a mistake to assume San Francisco will collapse under its own weight on any schedule at all; cleaning its streets, in his telling, is closer to negotiating with a demon. The city is the empire in miniature, and Spengler’s rootless city dweller, clever and practical and sterile, is its citizen.
From there the lecture traced the imperial line. Rome sits in a moral gray zone, neither Babylon nor the New Jerusalem, and its inheritance passed to Byzantium and then to the Holy Roman Empire, which Napoleon dissolved while his contemporaries compared him to the Antichrist. The War of the Spanish Succession was the last real attempt at a Catholic superpower, illustrated with Southey’s “The Battle of Blenheim,” and once the Bourbon and Habsburg question was settled, Catholic universalism never again organized Europe. Britain took the mantle, adopting Hobbes’s
Leviathan
as a sea power while keeping the philosopher at arm’s length, and Tennyson’s “Locksley Hall” imagined a federation of the world that bound the British to their American successors. Truman carried that poem in his pocket.
America, in Thiel’s reading, copied Rome on purpose.
The Federalist Papers
authors wrote under Latin names, Washington sits on the currency the way Augustus once did, and the United States may be the only truly sovereign country left, with a president who functions as the mayor of the United States and the dictator of everyone else by way of the Five Eyes alliance, Echelon, SWIFT, and the reserve dollar.
Then came the three tests. A New Rome must be the military superpower, the iron kingdom of Daniel 2:40. It must be the financial superpower that controls the rails on which the world buys and sells, which is Revelation 13:17 read as infrastructure. And it must be the ideological or religious superpower that wins hearts and minds at global scale, the territory Matthew 24:24 warns about. Russia fails the test in his view; Moscow is not Constantinople’s heir, the Bolshevik break makes the line hard to trace, and he could not say whether Putin is more a Christian or more a former KGB man.
The last movement was about violence, and then about the room he spoke in.
The United States keeps the death penalty, stands apart from the developed world for it, and polices itself as hard as it polices everyone else, which Thiel read as proof that Americans have not been gelded and have not handed off their katechonic duties. He landed the old line that America is the only country where a revolution is impossible because it is the only country without an American embassy. Then he turned to Nashville, called it the global capital of Protestantism, a religion with no center, and told the people in the seats to take responsibility at the level of ideas and institutions rather than wait for someone else to do the restraining. He kept his own caveat in place. The katechon restrains the man of lawlessness and carries, in the same body, the risk of becoming him.
The rails that restrain are the rails that would license, and the difference is the law under which the switch gets thrown.
Three tests, scored
Run the three tests against the world as it stands and the United States passes all of them, which is the uninteresting conclusion. The interesting one is where the margins differ, because the margins tell you which test is still being contested.

The world has not diversified how it pays, and Thiel’s second test is a test of how the world pays.

The dollar rail extending into software
Revelation 13:17, compiled
The stablecoin row is where the second element is being rebuilt in code. The GENIUS Act, signed July 18, 2025, requires one-for-one reserves in Treasury bills and cash, and Nellie Liang and Brent Neiman estimate at Brookings that first-round net demand for bills lands
between $400 billion and $2.3 trillion by 2030
, depending on whether the growth comes from abroad or from money funds that would sell the same bills to buy the coins. Either way, private digital dollars become a funding channel for the sovereign, which is the stated intent.
Tennessee’s junior senator, Bill Hagerty, wrote the law and
said in August
that he drafted it in part to guarantee dollar dominance over digital currencies and create structural demand for Treasuries.
The same paper names the tradeoff without flinching. If stablecoins route around correspondent banking, the leverage behind sanctions weakens unless issuers and wallets can block a transaction on a lawful order, so the authors recommend requiring exactly that. That is Revelation 13:17 stated as a compliance rule. Section 3(b)(2) of the Act takes effect January 18, 2027, platforms listing Tether need a documented basis to believe the issuer can freeze funds on a US order by then, and
no foreign jurisdiction
had received a Treasury comparability determination as of Monday, which happened to be the day the Treasury’s own comment window closed and the day Thiel took the stage.
The banking layer underneath it is being built by Thiel’s own circle.
Erebor, the Columbus bank that Palmer Luckey and Joe Lonsdale founded with Founders Fund behind it, and which 2PM covered in January as
the dragon-guarded mountain
, told the OCC it intends to be
the most regulated entity
conducting stablecoin transactions and
won its national charter
in February. The restrainer is extending its rail into software, the extension carries the license switch with it, and the capital behind the extension shares a cap table with the lecturer.
Why Nashville
Thiel’s third test has no scoreboard, so he answered with a city, and he didn’t choose it at random.
Nashville has been called the buckle of the Bible Belt for generations: the Southern Baptist Convention’s executive committee and Lifeway sit in the metro, Thomas Nelson has published Bibles from the city for decades, the Gospel Music Association and the Christian music industry are headquartered there, and The Daily Wire moved its operation to Nashville in 2020.
A religion with no center still has a supply chain, and most of the American Protestant supply chain runs through one metro area.
The venue carried the same message.
The Pamphleteer
positions itself against corporate and progressive media, Meriwether Academy exists to build fraternal networks and boys’ schools, and the opening night was held in a Masonic lodge that
opened the evening
by distancing itself from the content. Thiel’s charge to the room was that the people who already own the ideas and institutions should act like the restrainer rather than wait for one.
The financial rail runs through the same state, and that is the part of the final lecture that was not theology. Hagerty wrote the law that extends the dollar into software, and two of Thiel’s three elements, the religious infrastructure and the authorship of the dollar’s next rail, already have a Tennessee address. A man who tells a room to take responsibility for ideas and institutions is usually describing a room that has already started.
Restrainer or catalyst
The tools that make the United States
the katechon
are the tools Revelation assigns to the beast, and Thiel alluded to this. The 2022 freeze of Russia’s reserves and the removal of Russian banks from SWIFT were the restrainer deciding who may buy and sell, and the GENIUS requirement that issuers and wallets block transactions on a lawful order is the same decision compiled into software. What separates the restrainer from the thing it restrains is not the instrument. It is the law under which the instrument is used and the breadth of the hand that holds it.
His critics make the inverse case from the same facts. The Vatican-adjacent press in March called his vision a
superplutocracy
that would monitor humanity in the name of guarding it, and the standing objection to the series is that the man warning about surveillance
co-founded Palantir
, a company whose commercial logic we laid out in
The Drop Economy
. Both sides are describing one machine from different ends, and the argument is over who holds the keys.
Three conditions would turn the restrainer into the catalyst, and each one can be watched. The license switch moves from a sovereign exception to a programmable default inside wallets and checkouts. The three elements migrate from a national government to a supranational one, which is Thiel’s one world government drawn as an org chart. The rails get turned inward on domestic commerce rather than outward on adversaries, which is what the mayor of the United States looks like on the day the mayor decides he is also the dictator.
What operators should do?
The second element is the one commerce operators sit on, and the third is the one they actually own. Six moves follow from that.
Settlement is a choice, not a utility.
Shopify, Coinbase, and Stripe
put USDC on Base into Shopify Payments and Shop Pay in June 2025, with merchants paid out in local currency by default. A merchant that turns that on has joined the dollar rail’s software extension, and should know which rail it is on and under whose law it runs.
Sanctions screening is now a product feature.
The lawful-order obligations in GENIUS reach wallets and platforms on January 18, 2027, and OFAC exposure already sits at checkout for any brand selling across a border. Build the screening into the order flow rather than the finance team’s inbox.
Corridor risk is rail risk.
Hong Kong clears
76 percent
of offshore renminbi payments, and China-Russia trade settles mostly outside the dollar. A supply chain that touches those corridors is settling on a challenger rail whether or not the invoice says so.
Brands are the hearts-and-minds rail.
Thiel’s third element is the ideological contest at global scale, and the storefront is the surface where that contest reaches a consumer every day. A publication that sells and a store that publishes are the same operation, both of them carry a flag, and we argued in December that
defense itself is becoming a brand
for the same reason.
The restrainer funds itself through your float.
Every dollar a merchant holds in a GENIUS-compliant stablecoin is backed by a Treasury bill or cash in the issuer’s reserve, which means the operator’s working capital has become part of the sovereign’s balance sheet. Price that into treasury decisions rather than treating stablecoin balances as cash with a yield.
Nashville is a commerce market with a theology attached.
The audience Thiel addressed owns churches, schools, publishers, and music catalogs, and it buys like a market with its own supply chain. Operators building in faith-adjacent categories should read the final lecture as a demand signal rather than a sermon.

The verdict
The lecture will be read as theology, and the coverage will sort itself into the usual two piles: the billionaire with the Antichrist obsession, or the philosopher the press refuses to understand. Both readings miss the operative claim.
Thiel described the restrainer as a government that fields the strongest army, controls the rails of exchange, and wins the argument for hearts and minds, and then he looked at the room and told it that the third job was theirs.
On the numbers, the restrainer is intact. The army has been requested at a level the country has not funded since 1945, the rails carry half the world’s payment value and four-fifths of its trade finance, and the challenger rail has been losing share for two years. What has changed is where they’re building the rail. It is being built in software, under a Tennessee senator’s statute, on charters issued to banks from the lecturer’s own cap table, with a license switch written into the code because the Treasury’s economists asked for one.
The Katechon Economy is the business of holding the rails.
Commerce operators do not get to choose whether they are on them. They do get to choose whether they know it, and the last Nashville lecture, read as infrastructure rather than prophecy, is the clearest account anyone has given of what being on them now means.
Peter Thiel has held a New Zealand passport since 2011, and for the better part of two years he has been tied, by the press and by his own itinerary, to Argentina. On its face that is a strange posture for a man who spent four Monday nights telling Nashville that America is the New Rome, the katechon that holds back the man of lawlessness, and that the people in the seats should take responsibility for its ideas and institutions. It is less strange once the lecture is read as a thesis about rails rather than a sermon about a country. The katechon in Thiel’s telling is not a static good. It is the empire best positioned to become the thing it restrains, and a man who believes that keeps a second passport the way an operator keeps a second rail.
His geography is what it looks like to hedge the restrainer failing, and he has been hedging since before he had a name for it. He told Nashville to stay and build. But just in case, he has the exits.
