Best Ecommerce Marketing Agency: What CTC Does, How We Think, and What Makes Us Different

 by Common Thread Collective

 Nov. 19 2026

 On any given week, more than 170 ecommerce brands trust Common Thread Collective to manage their paid media, creative strategy, and growth planning. Those brands span 7-figure startups to 9-figure category leaders, and they collectively represent hundreds of millions in annual ad spend. If you are searching for the best ecommerce marketing agency, here is an honest account of what we do, how we think, and what makes us different.

What Does an Ecommerce Marketing Agency Actually Do?

The best ecommerce agencies do more than run ads. They build the operating system your brand needs to grow profitably. At CTC, that means managing paid media across Meta, Google, AppLovin, TikTok Shop, and Amazon, producing the creative that fuels those channels, forecasting the revenue those efforts will generate, and measuring the true incremental impact of every dollar spent.

Most agencies optimize for platform-reported ROAS. We optimize for contribution margin and profit. That is a meaningful difference. A campaign can show a strong ROAS in Meta Ads Manager while losing money at the business level. We build toward the number that matters: what your brand actually takes home after every cost is accounted for.

What Makes CTC Different From Other Ecommerce Agencies?

Three things set CTC apart from most ecommerce marketing agencies.

The dataset. We work with more than 170 brands simultaneously. That means we see patterns across categories, channels, and scale stages that no single-brand team ever could. When TikTok Shop changes its algorithm or Meta rolls out a new optimization, we see it happening in real time across our entire portfolio — and we adjust every brand accordingly.

The Prophit System. CTC built a proprietary operating system for ecommerce growth called the Prophit Engine. It connects your advertising data, your financial data, and your creative output into a single decision-making framework. Instead of managing Meta separately from Google separately from your P&L, everything runs through one unified view.

The Omni System. We built the first P&L-level forecast across dot-com, TikTok Shop, and Amazon that quantifies the true demand cascade between every sales channel and media channel in your business. Most brands are making multimillion-dollar budget decisions without understanding how their channels actually affect each other. The Omni System solves that.

What Results Do CTC's Clients Typically See?

Results vary by brand, category, and starting point. But across our portfolio, common outcomes include significant improvement in contribution margin, more efficient new customer acquisition, and growth that holds up under proper incrementality measurement, not just platform attribution.

Some specific examples: Ridge Wallet scaled from a small TikTok Shop presence to one of the top-performing brands on the platform. Cuts Clothing grew revenue profitably while improving blended efficiency across channels. Gruns scaled to 8 figures and was acquired. HexClad became one of the most recognizable cookware brands in the US. These are not the exception. They are what happens when you build the right operating system under a brand.

What Channels Does CTC Manage?

We manage the full demand cascade: every channel where your customers discover, consider, and purchase your product.

On the media side: Meta (Facebook and Instagram), Google (Search, Shopping, YouTube, Performance Max), AppLovin, TikTok Ads, Snapchat, and Amazon Advertising. On the sales channel side: Shopify dot-com, TikTok Shop, and Amazon. On the creative side: performance creative production, UGC strategy, and creative testing frameworks. On the measurement side: incrementality testing, media mix modeling, and P&L-level forecasting.

We do not manage every channel for every brand. We build the right channel mix for where your brand is in its growth stage and where the highest-leverage opportunities exist.

How Does CTC Approach Creative Strategy?

Creative is the highest-leverage variable in paid social performance. The difference between a median ad and a top-1% ad is not the audience or the bid strategy — it is the creative. Across 1.339 million ads analyzed across 158 brands, the median ad loses money (-$3.20 value). The top 5% of ads produce 73% of all value. The top 1% produce 41%.

Our creative strategy is built around volume, testing velocity, and identifying the rare winning concepts that drive outsized returns. We work with Tribe to scale creator-driven content production, dramatically reducing the cost per creative and increasing the number of tests run per quarter. More tests means more chances to find the ads that actually move the business.

What Size Brands Does CTC Work With?

CTC works primarily with 7-figure, 8-figure, and 9-figure ecommerce brands. Our engagements are most valuable for brands that are past product-market fit, running meaningful ad spend, and looking to build the infrastructure to scale profitably rather than just grow revenue.

If you are under $1M in annual revenue, we are probably not the right fit yet. If you are doing $5M or more and want to understand what a true growth operating system looks like for your business, that is exactly who we are built for.

How Do I Know If CTC Is the Right Ecommerce Agency for My Brand?

The honest answer is that we are not the right fit for every brand. We are the right fit if you care about profit, not just revenue. If you want your agency to understand your P&L, not just your ROAS. If you are willing to invest in a real operating system rather than a month-to-month ad management relationship.

We built the Omni System specifically for brands running across multiple sales channels who want a unified view of where demand is actually being created and captured — and a real plan to allocate resources into 2027. If that is the problem you are trying to solve, we should talk.
